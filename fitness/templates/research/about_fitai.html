About FitAI

FitAI is an adaptive physiological AI research platform integrating neural prediction systems, latent-state physiological modeling, stochastic recovery dynamics, OpenCV body analysis, and multidimensional physiological intelligence into a unified research architecture.

Physiological Intelligence
FitAI combines observable physiological signals, adaptive neural prediction systems, and latent-state modeling to simulate multidimensional physiological behavior.
Adaptive Neural Systems
The platform uses custom neural architectures, nonlinear physiological analysis, recovery optimization, and explainable AI validation systems.
Computer Vision Integration
OpenCV-based body proportion analysis enables physiological contour extraction, geometric body analysis, and adaptive physique estimation.
Research Architecture
The FitAI ecosystem integrates stochastic physiological dynamics, latent physiological state embeddings, Hamilton–Jacobi-inspired adaptive optimization, physiological signal analysis, and adaptive recovery intelligence into a unified physiological AI framework.

The system is designed to explore both observable physiological measurements and hidden physiological states through adaptive neural prediction, multidimensional monitoring, and nonlinear physiological modeling.
